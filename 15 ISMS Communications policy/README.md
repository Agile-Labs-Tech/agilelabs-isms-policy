# Purpose, Scope And Users

1. The purpose of this document is to define the procedures to communicate effectively to the management and staff involved in the ISMS.
2. It provides guidance for sharing the necessary information.
3. It also provides guidance for the comments or suggestions to be assessed and considered for continuous development of the ISMS.

1.
# Related Documents

1. ISO/IEC 27001 standard, clauses A.11.2.8 and A.11.2.9
2. Information Security Policy
3. List of Legal, Regulatory, Contractual and Other Requirements
4. External communications list

1.
# Roles &amp; Responsibilities

1. The Tech Leader (TL) is responsible to carry out the relevant communication to all level of staff as detailed procedure.
2. The TL shall be responsible to evaluate improvements suggested by the staff and if applicable, process the implementation of these suggestions.

1.
# Procedures

1.
## Internal Communication

1.
### The System Administrator (SA) shall ensure that the ISMS are well and clearly disseminated to all the employees of the organization. The SA shall implement the ISMS with the employees being fully aware of the factors involved in the efficiency energy use.
2.
### The **SA** shall communicate the essential elements of the ISMS to the employees in order to make it happen.
3.
### The information and implementation of ISMS can be given thru different type of modes, it may include, but not limited to, the following:

1. Induction and Briefing of Staff
2. Proficiency level of the skills as identified in the job criteria.
3. Email Correspondence
4. On Job Training

1.
### The information communication may include the following:

1. ISMS Policy
2. Information on ISMS Statement of Applicability.
3. The possibility of each individual to contribute towards ISMS Risk Assessment.
4. The conformance to legal and other requirements
5. Contact person for further details
6. Areas for improvement

1.
### The above information shall be communicated to the employees on a regular interval, as stated in the table below. Information shall be communicated to the employee via email or internal notice board.

| **Item** | **Mode of Communication** | **Frequency** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISMS Policy | Orientation, Email | Once, Yearly or When there is changes in Policy |
| Monthly SLA Report to Customer | Email | Monthly |
| SOA | Orientation, Email | Once, Yearly or When there are changes in Policy |
| Other items related to ISMS | Briefing, Email | As Required |

###


1.
## External Communications

1. A table listing the external parties and the types of communications with these parties, with the employee or team responsible for the communications will be maintained by the SA.

1. Should there be a need to communicate with any other external organizations; the TL shall be responsible to ensure that the requirements are addressed.

1.
## Continual Improvement

1. Whenever applicable, all personnel are encouraged to provide suggestions to the improvement of the ISMS.
2. The relevant suggestions shall be given to the TL during the &quot;Management Meetings&quot;.
3. The TL shall review the proposal and determine if the improvements are applicable and practicable for implementation.
4. The SA shall collate the implementation of the improvements and it should be updated in the Management Review Meeting.
