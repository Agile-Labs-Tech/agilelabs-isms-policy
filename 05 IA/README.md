# Purpose, Scope And Users

1. The purpose of this procedure is to describe all audit-related activities – preparing the audit plan, selecting an auditor, conducting individual audits and reporting.
2. This procedure is applicable to all activities related to the Information Security Management System (ISMS) implemented and maintained by Agile Lab Pte Ltd (Agile Lab).
3. Users of this document are members of top management of Agile Lab; as well as internal auditors.

1.
# Reference Documents

1. ISO/IEC 27001 standard, clause 9.2
2. Information Security Policy
3. Procedure for Corrective Action

1.
# Validity And Document Management

1. This document is valid as of date stated on the cover of this document.
2. The owner of this document is System Administrator (SA), who must check and, if necessary, update the document at least once a year.
3. When evaluating the effectiveness and adequacy of this document, the following criteria need to be considered:

1. number of corrective actions identified during the audit
2. number of corrective actions identified during the certification audit conducted after the internal audit
3. whether the internal audit frequency is in line with the Annual Internal Audit Program

# Internal Audit

  1.
## Purpose of internal audit

1. The purpose of internal audit is to determine whether procedures, controls, processes, arrangements and other activities within the ISMS are in line with ISO 27001 standard, applicable regulations, and the organization&#39;s internal documentation, whether they are effectively implemented and maintained and whether they meet policy requirements and set objectives.

  1.
## Internal audit planning

1. The Risk Management Committee (RMC) shallapprove an annual program for internal audits, written as outlined in the form in Appendix 1.
2. One or more internal audits should be conducted in the course of one year, ensuring cumulative coverage of the entire ISMS scope.
3. Internal audits are planned based on risk assessment, as well as results of previous audits; they are usually conducted before management review.
4. The annual internal audit program has to contain the following information about each individual internal audit:

1. period of the audit (specifying dates, or month in which the audit is planned)
2. scope of the audit (departments, processes, clauses of the standard, etc.)
3. audit criteria (standards, legislation and regulations, internal documentation, corporate standards, and/or contractual obligations)
4. auditing methods (review of documentation, interviews with employees, review of records, review of information systems, etc.)
5. who will be conducting the audit (if there is more than one auditor, specify the audit team leader)

1. Audits conducted shall be recorded in the annual internal audit program.

  1.
## Appointing internal auditors

1. The Head of RMC shall appoint internal auditors.
2. An internal auditor may be someone from the organization or a person outside the organization.
3. The following criteria shall be taken into consideration when appointing internal auditors:

1. knowledge of ISO/IEC 27001 standard
2. familiarity with management system auditing techniques
3. knowledge of how information and communication technologies function to the extent that he/she is familiar with the purpose of individual systems as well as the impacts on security processes and/or on business continuity

1. Internal auditors must be selected in such a way as to ensure objectivity and impartiality. This is to avoid conflict of interest. For example, auditors are not allowed to audit their own work.

  1.
## Conducting individual internal audits

1. Persons responsible for individual internal audits are identified in the Annual Internal Audit Program.
2. If an audit is conducted by a team consisting of several auditors, the person responsible for the audit is the one identified as Audit Team Leader.
3. The following must be taken into consideration during an internal audit:

1. criteria laid down in the Annual Internal Audit Program
2. results of previous internal or external audits
3. results of risk assessment, controls implementation, business impact analysis, etc.
4. Internal audit checklist – see Appendix 3

1. The following must be documented as internal audit results:

1. Internal Audit Report – it must be sent to TL
2. possible corrective actions must be documented in the Corrective Action Form, as required by the Procedure for Corrective Action
