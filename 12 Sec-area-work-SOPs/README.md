# Purpose, Scope And Users

1. The purpose of this document is to define basic rules of behavior in the secure areas.
2. This document is applied to all secure areas in the Information Security Management System (ISMS).
3. Users of this document are all employees of Agile Lab Pte Ltd (Agile Lab).

# Rules For Secure Areas

## List of secure areas

1. Existing secure areas that require special rules are the following:
    1. Office

2. Responsible persons for each secure area are listed as asset owners in the Inventory of Assets.

## Right of access to secure areas

1. Access to secure areas is approved according to the Information Security Policy.

## Entry controls

1. Access to secure areas is protected with the following entry controls:
    1. Door keys
    2. CCTV cameras

## Access of visitors

1. Persons who are not employed in Agile Lab (that is, visitors) must obtain permission to access by Agile Lab Management.
2. Visitors may enter the secure areas and stay in those areas only in the presence of a designated employee – this employee must accompany the visitor throughout their whole stay in the secure area.

## Prohibited activities

1. In secure areas it is not allowed to:
    1. perform any kind of photographic, audio or video recording;
    2. connect any device to a network unless specifically authorized to do so;
    3. archive a larger amount of paper materials;
    4. store flammable materials or equipment;
    5. use any kind of heating devices;
